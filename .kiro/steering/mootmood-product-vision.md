# MootMood Product Vision & Core Principles

**Product Name:** MootMood  
**Tagline:** Search Smarter. Plead Stronger.  
**Category:** International Law Legal Research & Moot Court Platform

## Product Identity

MootMood is a professional legal research platform designed specifically for:
- International law students
- Moot court participants
- Legal researchers
- Academics
- International law practitioners

## Core Problem Statement

International law mooters struggle to:
1. Find relevant international court judgments and decisions
2. Identify authoritative legal sources supporting specific legal propositions
3. Locate specific paragraphs within lengthy judgments
4. Discover cases through legal topics rather than case names alone
5. Extract relevant legal passages efficiently
6. Generate accurate citations according to different citation styles
7. Organize legal authorities for memorials and legal arguments

## Core User Journey

**Search by legal issue → Discover authoritative sources → Identify relevant paragraphs → Extract passages → Generate accurate citations**

## Key Differentiators

Unlike general search engines, MootMood prioritizes:
- **Legal concepts and principles** over simple keyword matching
- **Topic-based discovery** through hierarchical legal classification
- **Paragraph-level precision** with pinpoint references
- **Citation accuracy** with multiple style support
- **Research organization** tailored to moot court preparation

## Design Philosophy

- **Professional over flashy:** Look like a legal research tool, not a student project
- **Accuracy over quantity:** Verified sources trump large, unreliable datasets
- **Structured over chaotic:** Organized taxonomy beats keyword chaos
- **Traceable over mysterious:** Always show source provenance
- **Reliable over experimental:** Build on stable foundations

## Target User Experience

When a user searches for "Non-intervention", they should discover:
- Cases discussing prohibition of intervention
- Coercion principles
- State sovereignty doctrines
- Intervention by invitation
- Support for armed opposition groups
- Prohibition of indirect intervention

With direct access to relevant paragraphs in authoritative judgments.

## Long-term Vision

Build a platform that international law mooters genuinely want to use throughout their research and memorial preparation - making legal research faster, more organized, and more effective.
