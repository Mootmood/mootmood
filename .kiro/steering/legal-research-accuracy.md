# Legal Research Accuracy & Source Integrity

## Critical Principle

MootMood is an academic legal research platform. **Accuracy must take priority over the quantity of search results.**

## Mandatory Source Rules

### 1. Official Sources First
- Prioritize official court and tribunal websites
- Prefer official judgments and treaty repositories
- Always retain source URLs and pinpoint references
- Include source provenance and verification dates where practical

### 2. Never Fabricate Legal Content
NEVER invent:
- Case names
- Judgments or decisions
- Paragraph numbers
- Quotations
- Treaty provisions
- Citations
- Legal topic classifications without supporting authority

### 3. Preserve Original Legal Text
- Maintain original wording of legal documents
- Clearly differentiate quotations, summaries, and editorial explanations
- Never present AI-generated legal explanations as authoritative judicial statements

### 4. Transparency Requirements
- Clearly identify uncertainty or incomplete source verification
- Keep primary authorities and secondary sources distinguishable
- Mark data extraction dates and verification status
- Distinguish between different document types (judgments, orders, advisory opinions)

### 5. Legal and Ethical Boundaries
- Respect copyright and website terms of use
- Do not scrape restricted databases
- Do not circumvent access controls
- Do not reproduce copyrighted material without valid legal basis
- Where full-text reproduction is not permitted, retain metadata and link to official document

### 6. Metadata Integrity
For every legal authority, maintain structured metadata including:
- Full case name and short name
- Court/tribunal
- Parties
- Date
- Document type
- Official citation
- Official source URL
- Full-text availability status
- Source verification status

### 7. Paragraph-Level Accuracy
For judgment paragraphs:
- Preserve original paragraph numbers
- Maintain exact text
- Retain page numbers where available
- Link to parent document
- Mark source location

## Quality Over Quantity

Start with 10–20 carefully verified case records rather than a large, unreliable database.

Each included case must link to its official source.

## Important Disclaimer

MootMood is an academic research tool, not a substitute for independent legal analysis.

Users must verify authorities and conduct their own legal research.

## Terminology Precision

- Do not conflate distinct legal concepts
- Maintain proper distinctions between related doctrines
- Example: Pre-emptive self-defence ≠ Peremptory norms (jus cogens)
- Use established international law terminology
